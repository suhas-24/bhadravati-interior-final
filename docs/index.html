Boards & hardware

Lock from BOARD_DECISION.md (full-field catalog + reviews; Sainik-wardrobe rec revisited). Kitchen = shutters-only on existing granite. Wardrobe = carcass + three Idria leaves. Recessed brushed stainless pulls stay locked. Name “710” ≠ IS 710 marine.

Kitchen shutters (S1241 MT Latte 0.8 mm · 2 mm E3 ABS · 3000 K): Century Club Prime 19 mm with IS 710 on the sheet-edge stamp + QR. Alternate: Action Tesa Boilo 18 mm if CNC/HDF-fluent, or Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm (IS 710 stamp). Do not default to Sainik 710 (official page = IS 303 BWP, not marine). Laminate both faces; seal every edge.
Wardrobe (54×90×19.20 in · three Idria Oak leaves · 1 mm E3 ABS): Club Prime 19 mm or Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm. CNC alternate: HDHMR / HDWR 18 mm. Boilo too heavy for 90 in doors. Sainik 710 19 mm = contingency only (QR, no core gaps) — not the written default. Never MR, particle board, MDF, or unbranded “710”.
