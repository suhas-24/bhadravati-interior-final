03 Kannada / English Call Script

Five questions, one call. If Q1–Q2 both land yes, ask the rest — otherwise thank them and move down the strip.

Kannada / English Call Script

Lead in Kannada; numbers and rates usually switch to English on their own.

  1. “ನಿಮ್ಮ ಅಂಗಡಿಯಲ್ಲಿ HOT PRESS ಇದೆಯಾ?” “Do you run a hot press?” then: platen ≥ 8×4 ft?
  2. “Press machine ಬೇರೊಬ್ಬರ board-laminate JOB WORK ಮಾಡಿ ಕೊಡ್ತೀರಾ?” “Will you do board-laminate job work for outside customers?”
  3. Rate per sheet / sqft? Minimum lot size? Glue — our own or yours (we bring Fevicol HI-PER)?
  4. Panel saw / scoring blade available? Rate? Edge bander on site? ₹ per metre?
  5. “Machine ತೋರಿಸ್ತೀರಾ?” “Will you show me the machine?” refusal = red flag — no machine tour, no order.
