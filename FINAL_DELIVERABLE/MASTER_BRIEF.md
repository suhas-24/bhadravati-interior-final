**Role:** Lead Interior Designer — authoritative proposal  
**Location:** Bhadravati, Karnataka (first-floor studio)  
**FINAL concept:** **Warm Contemporary Minimalism — climate-resilient**  
**Document status:** Design control package for client + Visual Producer. **Not fabrication-approved** until signed site measure and physical sample approval.

Companion files: `01_Design_Direction.md` — `07_Risks_Assumptions_Verify.md`, `design_tokens.json`.

## 1. Design direction and problems solved

The home will feel calm, practical, and locally believable — not a pale decorative showroom and not textbook Japandi. Architecture and usable circulation come before styling.

**Problems solved:** dust/smoke visibility; hard-water streaking; monsoon humidity at wet joinery; glare under strong daylight; repairability; and prior visual QA failures (wrong openings, flipped kitchen, invented cabinetry, false gloss, unsafe clearances, climate-mismatched props).

**Non-negotiables**

- Existing **black granite** kitchen remains; **shutters only**.
- Kitchen base + drawers + loft = **Century S1241 MT Latte** only.
- Hardware = **brushed stainless recessed** pulls (J-pull / finger-pull) only — no projecting bar handles.
- Lighting = **3000 K** warm-neutral baseline.
- Finishes = **matte / low-sheen**.
- No TV feature wall, island, L-kitchen flip, gold strips, slatted Japandi props, or gloss/sparkle laminates.

**Concepts compared (max 3) — one winner**

| | Concept | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Warm Contemporary Minimalism (NN9074 + Latte kitchen + Idria wardrobe) | **FINAL** |
| B | Textbook Japandi | Reject as governing style |
| C | Dual-tone kitchen / lighter default walls (as in some older handover renders) | Reject |

## 7. QA compliance (Visual_QA_Critique)

Every critical room delivery must use a **three-part set**: (1) plan-faithful wide view, (2) opposite/adjacent view proving orientation/circulation, (3) close material/detail view. Label room, camera, orientation. Mark unknowns **not verified**.

Reject visuals that: move/mirror openings; flip kitchen; invent island/lofts/TV walls; show dual kitchen colours; gloss granite/laminate; use two huge wardrobe doors; block swings/plumbing; add Japandi prop clutter.
