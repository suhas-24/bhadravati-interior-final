# 04 — Lighting Plan

**Baseline CCT:** **3000 K** warm white for living and sleeping zones (acceptable bedroom bedside range **2700–3000 K**). Avoid cool-white house-wide. Light should read warm-neutral — **not orange, not dim cave**.

**CRI:** Prefer **Ra ?90** where colour of paint/laminate/food matters (kitchen task, living ambient near finishes).

---

## Layer strategy

| Layer | Intent | Typical devices |
|---|---|---|
| **Ambient** | General orientation, evening comfort | Ceiling diffused downlights or surface fixtures on dimmers; bounce light preferred over harsh spots |
| **Task** | Work, cook, read, groom | Kitchen under-loft linear LEDs; desk lamp; bedside lamps; mirror light at basin if added later |
| **Accent** | Quiet hierarchy only | Optional soft wash on wardrobe grain or one art — **no gold strips, niche glitter, or fake cove everywhere** |
| **Daylight control** | Glare, heat, privacy, evening transition | Breathable curtains / light sheers + practical blinds at west/east openings as needed |

---

## Lux guidance (design targets, not photometric cert)

| Activity / zone | Target lux | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Living general | **150–300** | Even ambient; dimmable |
| Reading / detail | **300–500** | Floor/table lamp or sofa-side task |
| Kitchen worktops | **300–750** | Continuous under-loft strip; avoid glare into eyes |
| Bedroom general | **50–150** | Soft ambient + separate bedside task |
| Study desk | **300–500** | Task lamp + daylight; avoid screen reflections |
| Bathroom general | **150–300** | Moisture-rated fittings; control face shadows at basin |

---

## Room-by-room

### Living

- Ambient: 2–4 dimmable 3000 K downlights or equivalent diffused fixtures (exact count after reflected ceiling plan on verified layout).
- Task/accent: one floor or table lamp near sofa for reading.
- TV: avoid bright fixtures aimed at screen; keep wall around TV mid-tone (NN9074) to reduce glare perception.
- Daylight: preserve openings; no tall furniture blocking windows.

### Kitchen

- **Mandatory task:** linear LED under loft / under lower shelf line aimed at granite worktop (3000 K, high CRI, diffuser profile — not bare diodes).
- Ambient: one–two ceiling points so fridge and aisle are safe without relying only on task strip.
- Do not create orange cast on S1241 Latte — test LED brand with laminate sample.
- Keep fixtures clear of loft shutter swings and cleaning reach.

### Bedroom

- Ambient low (50–150 lux).
- Two bedside lamps **2700–3000 K**, switched separately.
- Optional soft ceiling fixture on dimmer.
- No cool “hospital” downlight grid.

### Study

- Daylight primary from large north window (verify size/orientation).
- LED task lamp on desk (articulated or fixed arm), 3000 K or switchable 3000/4000 if client needs cool for long work — default evening still 3000 K ambient.
- Avoid glossy desk top that mirrors window.

### Wardrobe / dressing

- Dedicated 3000 K linear or spot so Idria Oak grain and clothing colours are true; place so open leaves do not shadow the hanging bay.
- Do not rely on distant living ambient alone.

### Bathroom

- IP-rated / moisture-appropriate fittings.
- Vertical or flanking light at basin preferred over single overhead that caves eye sockets.
- Exhaust + window ventilation preserved.

---

## Controls & glare

- Separate **switching zones**: living ambient / kitchen task / bedroom / study.
- Dimming on living + bedroom ambient.
- Use shielded or diffused optics; no sparkle laminates that amplify grazing highlights.
- Bright windows: do not “clip” in future visuals; design real sheers for daytime glare.

---

## Electrical assumptions (verify on site)

- Confirm existing switchboard locations; do **not** conceal panels with new joinery.
- Add kitchen task circuit and wardrobe light points if missing — engage licensed electrician.
- No structural/electrical certification implied by this design brief.
