## Undertones & appearance context

| Finish | Undertone / behaviour | Under 3000 K evening | Maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|
| NN9088 | Light warm ecru | Reads creamier and brighter under 3000 K | Use washable system; wipe schedule stricter |
| WW0020 | Warm virgin white | Soft warm white; not blue-white | Ceiling/trim: low touch |
| S1241 MT Latte | Warm beige solid matte | Must **not** turn orange under warm LEDs — sample-check | Wipeable; matte hides hard-water haze better than gloss |
| 80236 DW | Deep graphite woodgrain | Can read heavy beside black granite — limit to TV cabinet | Wipe with grain; avoid polish that creates glare |
| 83661 SU Sonoma Oak | Light warm-neutral oak | Quiet vertical grain; keeps wardrobe from darkening the room | Soft cloth; no silicone shine sprays |
| Black granite | Cool-black with natural fleck | Restrained sheen only | Hard water: wipe dry after use |

**Metamerism:** Latte + NN9074 + granite + oak will shift between daylight and 3000 K. Sample board mandatory.

## Sheen schedule

| Surface | Sheen |
|---|---|
| Walls | Low-sheen / matt washable emulsion (prefer Birla Opus Calista Ever Wash or One Pure Elegance Matt class for scrub resistance — confirm local SKU) |
| Ceiling | Matt |
| Kitchen / wardrobe laminates | **MT / SU matte–soft** as coded; no GL/CX gloss families |
| Hardware | Brushed (diffuse), not mirror |
| Granite | Existing finish retained; do not re-polish to mirror for “luxury” look |

## E3 edge-band evidence (from profile extract)

- Materials: PVC & ABS; finishes include Super Matt / Soft Touch families — select **matt/soft** match, not gloss/sparkle.
- Standard thicknesses include **1.00 mm** and **2.00 mm** options — align to kitchen 2 mm / wardrobe 1 mm.
- **Approve only a physical edge sample matched to the exact laminate.** Do not order generic “Slate Grey” or “Frosty White” by name alone.

---

## Paint system guidance (Birla Opus interior notes)

- Tint in Birla Opus INTERIOR emulsions (One / Calista / Style ranges).
- Prefer washable + anti-fungal wall systems for monsoon humidity.
- Prep: quality putty + primer per brand system.
- Accent textures (Stone / Sand Dune) are **optional and limited** — default is flat quiet walls.
