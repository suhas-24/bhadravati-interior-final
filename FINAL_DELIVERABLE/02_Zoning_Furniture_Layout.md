### Shutter strategy (QA-critical)

- Use **3 doors = 1 single + 1 double** (3 leaves total) across the W-01 clear opening **1372 mm**  not four leaves, not two full-height slabs.
- Locked leaf widths **457 / 457 / 458 mm** (single L + double R) summing to **1372 mm** clear — see `coordination/wardrobe_door_lock.json` / dimension drawings; recessed handles non-dimensional; fabricator overlay/inset TBD after site measure.
- **Recessed** pulls (J-pull / finger-pull) on wardrobe faces; face options (A) aluminium + fluted glass, (B) plywood.
- Door straightener where fabricator shutter-size calc requires it.
- Allow ?**600 mm** standing clear in front of wardrobe for dressing; prefer ?900 mm if this aisle is also primary circulation.
