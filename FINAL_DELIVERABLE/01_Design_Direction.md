# 01 — Design Direction

**Project:** Bhadravati Home (first-floor studio)  
**Authoritative concept (FINAL):** Warm Contemporary Minimalism — climate-resilient  
**Status:** Design control package. Not fabrication-approved until signed site measure + physical sample approval.

---

## Chosen concept (one primary)

| Concept | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A — Warm Contemporary Minimalism (FINAL)** | **Adopt.** Calm greige architecture, one kitchen laminate, one wardrobe wood, brushed stainless, 3000 K light, no decorative TV wall / Japandi props. |
| B — Textbook Japandi | Reject as governing style. Optional calm DNA only; no slats, ash overload, bamboo props, or empty showroom styling. |
| C — Dual-tone kitchen + lighter walls (as in some prior handover renders) | Reject. Split base/loft colour and NN9088-as-default walls fail dust/darkness control beside black granite and contradict the final material brief. |

**Concept A solves:** dust/smoke visibility, hard-water streaking, monsoon humidity risk at wet zones, glare under strong daylight, repairability, and prior AI-render failures (wrong openings, flipped kitchen, invented cabinetry, glossy materials, unsafe clearances).

---

## Problems solved (maps to Visual QA Critique)

| QA failure class | How Concept A resolves it |
|---|---|
| Wrong window/door positions | Design and future visuals must lock to measured plan/photos; no mirrored rooms; openings listed as verify-on-site. |
| Wrong kitchen orientation | Single-wall existing granite only; sink under kitchen window; fridge at extreme right of kitchen zone; shutters only — no new carcass/island/counter. |
| Extra cabinetry / invented furniture | Approved inventory only (see §Approved elements). Empty wall ? storage opportunity. |
| Incorrect dimensions / scale | All fabrication sizes from `dimension_register_v1` (clear openings) + site re-measure; wardrobe = **four** narrower leaves on 54×90 in opening. |
| Glossy / false materials | Matte/low-sheen laminates and paint; black granite remains black granite; grain direction vertical on shutters. |
| Impossible circulation | Primary paths 900–1200 mm; furniture placed to keep door swings, sink access, fridge door, and wardrobe leaf arcs free. |
| Climate mismatch | Mid-tone greige walls, wipeable joinery, limited open shelving, sealed wet-risk boards, plumbing access retained. |

---

## Non-negotiable anchors

1. Existing kitchen **black granite** stays; design covers **shutters only**.
2. Kitchen base + drawer + loft = **one code**: Century StarLine **S1241 MT Latte**.
3. Hardware = **brushed stainless** only (no black / brass / mirror chrome mix).
4. Lighting baseline = **3000 K** warm-neutral (not orange, not cool white).
5. Finishes = **matte / low-sheen**; no wet acrylic, sparkle, or high-contrast marble drama.
6. Palette quiet: warm greige walls, Latte joinery, one oak wardrobe tone, optional one muted green accent maximum.
7. Japandi props, gold strips, TV feature walls, marble panels, and decorative slats are **out of scope**.

---

## Style DNA (observable decisions)

- **Silhouette:** Straight, quiet, floating or flush joinery; minimal reveals.
- **Wood tone:** One European-grey oak on wardrobe only (**84689 SU Idria Oak**); not repeated as pale ash everywhere.
- **Metal:** Brushed stainless throughout wet and dry zones.
- **Pattern scale:** Small/quiet woodgrain; no bold marble or sparkle laminates.
- **Textile hand:** Tight-weave, wipeable or washable upholstery; avoid bouclé dust traps.
- **Negative space:** Prefer empty wall over decorative fill.
- **Ornament density:** Near-zero; atmosphere from light, proportion, and finish quality.

---

## Climate brief (Bhadravati)

Dust/smoke, red laterite dust, hard water, hot summers, monsoon humidity, strong daylight, cross-ventilation, existing granite construction. Design response: mid-tone walls, low glare, wipeable fronts, BWP/WPC at wet risk, sealed edges, serviceable hardware, spare laminate retained.
