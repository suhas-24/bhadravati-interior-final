# Bhadravati kitchen + wardrobe — board / core options catalog

**Status:** Research catalog for specification. **Not a purchase order. Do not treat as fabrication-approved.**  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Job:** First-floor studio, Bhadravati, Karnataka. Kitchen = **shutters only** on existing black granite (K-01). Wardrobe = three hinged leaves in niche ~54 × 90 × 19.20 in (W-01). Locked faces: Century **S1241 MT Latte** (kitchen) and **84689 Idria Oak** (wardrobe Option B) + E3 ABS.  
**Rule:** Do **not** default kitchen or wardrobe to Century Sainik 710. Sainik is documented here as one budget BWP option with a naming trap.  
**Climate:** Inland Karnataka monsoon humidity + termites. Steam at hob/sink. Bedroom wardrobe is drier than kitchen but still mopped at plinth.

**How to read this file**

- **Official** = manufacturer product page, BIS product manual, or mill spec sheet.  
- **Marketing** = brand blogs, “72-hour boil”, ViroKill, Firewall, 200-hour boil, “far better than plywood”.  
- **Retailer / SEO** = dealer blogs, IndiaMART, HomeRun, hdhmr.in price lists. Useful for bands; not certificates.  
- **Anecdote** = Quora / carpenter lore. Use only as execution-risk flags.

## 1. Get the standard right (BIS, not the brand name)

The number **710 printed on a brand** is not the same as **IS 710 stamped by BIS**.

| Standard | Official title | What it actually covers | Glue (as required) | Typical Indian marketing misuse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IS 303 : 2024** | Plywood for **general purposes** (fourth revision) | Grades **MR / BWR / BWP**. Appearance types AA/AB/BB. Formaldehyde class **E1 or E2**. Nominal thicknesses include 15 / 19 mm (16 and 18 mm are commercial extras). | Manufacturer must declare resin type. MR is typically UF; BWR often MUF/PF; **BWP under IS 303 is still general-purpose plywood**, not marine craft ply. | Calling any “710” product “marine IS 710”. |
| **IS 710 : 2024** | **Marine plywood** (third revision) | Plywood for marine / river craft, pontoons, **prolonged moisture**. Tests include glue adhesion, water resistance, **tensile**, **static bending**, **wet bending**, mycological, **preservative retention**, formaldehyde. | Adhesive must be **BWP type of IS 848**. BIS product manual: *“The adhesives used for bonding the veneers shall be based on any suitable synthetic resin conforming to BWP Type of IS 848.”* | Using “IS 710” as a synonym for “waterproof kitchen ply”. |
| **IS 848** | Synthetic resin adhesives for plywood | Defines **BWP-type** phenolic (and related) adhesives. | Phenolic BWP type; IS 710:2010 text (still widely cited) forbids extender in the glue by the plywood mill; fillers ?10% of solid content. | “Phenolic” sticker without IS 848 type. |
| **IS 5509** | Fire-retardant plywood | FR **plywood** test regime. | n/a | Action Tesa claims Boilo HDF “confirming to IS:5509”. That is a **manufacturer claim for an HDF board**, not automatic BIS marking of plywood. Attach the test report. |
| **IS 10701** | Structural plywood | Load-bearing / structural. Greenply Club 700 official page leads with this. | n/a | Sold as “kitchen marine” without checking whether the **sheet stamp** is 10701, 710, or 303. |
| **IS 12406 : 2021** | MDF | Grade 1 (exterior / moisture-resistant class in manufacturer tables) vs Grade 2 (interior). HDHMR / HDWR products are usually sold against this family, not IS 710. | Typically **MUF** for moisture-resistant grades; UF for cheap interior MDF. | Calling HDHMR “BWP marine plywood”. It is **not plywood**. |
| **IS 3087 / IS 12823** | Particle board / prelam PB | Furniture-grade chipboard. | UF | Cheap modular kitchens. **Reject for this job.** |
| **IS 5539** | Preservative treatment of plywood | Greenply cites this for borer/termite treatment on Ecotec / Marine. | n/a | Treatment claim ? marine stamp. |

**Primary BIS sources (not marketing):**

- IS 303 product manual (grades **MR / BWR / BWP**): [https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-IS-303-_July-24.pdf](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-IS-303-_July-24.pdf)  
- IS 710 product manual (marine; **IS 848 BWP adhesive**; wet bending + preservative retention): [https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-IS-710-_July-24.pdf](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-IS-710-_July-24.pdf)  
- BIS plywood compendium (titles of IS 303:2024 and IS 710:2024): [https://services.bis.gov.in/tmp/compendium_2025-06-02-04-13-12.pdf](https://services.bis.gov.in/tmp/compendium_2025-06-02-04-13-12.pdf)

**Why IS 303 now has a BWP grade (2024):** BIS added BWP to general-purpose plywood so mills could sell boiling-waterproof **interior** boards without pretending they are marine craft plywood. Industry coverage: [Ply Reporter — BWP under IS 303](https://www.plyreporter.com/article/153905/the-new-bis-era-bwp-grade-comes-under-is-303); [Ply Insight on IS 303:2024](https://plyinsight.com/mastering-is-303-strategic-insights-into-plywood-testing-standards-and-industry-impact-part-3/). Century’s own Sainik disclaimer matches this: **“Asli” = IS 303 BWP (general purpose plywood)** — not IS 710.

**Practical hierarchy for this house**

1. **Kitchen shutters (steam, wipe-down, daily hinge cycles):** prefer **true IS 710-stamped plywood** *or* **Boilo BWP HDF** with fully sealed edges. IS 303 BWP is a fallback, not the first spec.  
2. **Wardrobe leaves (tall, 90 in, drier room):** calibrated **IS 303 BWP or IS 710 ply**, or **HDHMR 18 mm**. Avoid ultra-dense Boilo on full-height doors (weight / sag).  
3. **Never:** MR (UF), particle board, interior MDF, unbranded “710” with no ISI + CML on the **sheet edge**.

**Thickness note:** BIS lists 15 / 19 mm. Indian yards sell **16 mm** and **18 / 19 mm**. For shutters specify **18 mm HDHMR/Boilo** or **19 mm plywood** (or 18 mm if the mill actually produces it and you mic the sheet). Do not cut 16 mm kitchen doors.

**E0 vs E1 vs E2:** BIS plywood manuals licence **E1 / E2**, not E0. **E0 / CARB / “zero formaldehyde”** on Indian ply sites is a **manufacturer extra claim**. Do not print E0 on the client PDF unless the **batch mill certificate** is in the job file.

## 2. Job-specific performance (what the board must do)

| Demand | Kitchen shutters | Wardrobe 3-door |
|---|---|---|
| Moisture | Steam + sink splash + monsoon. BWP-class glue or Boilo. | Humidity + mopping at plinth. BWP or HDHMR with sealed edges. |
| Hinge cups | Daily cycle. Need **gap-free / composed core** or homogeneous HDF. | Tall leaves: 4–5 quality cups; core gaps tear out. |
| Weight | Moderate door sizes (B1 48 in / B2 36 in / B3 18 in). Boilo OK. | ~90 in leaves. Density >1000 kg/m³ is a sag/hinge tax. |
| Face | 0.8 mm Latte + **2 mm ABS**. Calibrated board preferred. | 0.8 mm Idria + **1 mm ABS**. Calibrated board preferred. |
| Termite | GLP / fibre process + site treatment. | Same. |
| Execution | If carpenter has never edged HDF, use calibrated **plywood** instead of a first-time Boilo experiment. | Traditional ply is lower process risk for site-built niche doors. |

## 4. HDF / HDHMR options

These are **fibreboards**, not plywood. Moisture-**resistant** (except Boilo’s BWP claim). Edges must be **ABS/PVC sealed**; failed edges swell **irreversibly**. Excellent for **calibrated 0.8 mm laminate**. Hinge cups in homogeneous core are generally more reliable than alternate-core ply — **if** the shop uses the right bits, cups, and screws.

## 5. MDF and particle board — why reject for this job

| Product | Typical glue | Why it fails here |
|---|---|---|
| **Interior MDF** (IS 12406 Grade 2) | UF | Swells with kitchen steam and monsoon; poor wet screw holding; edges explode if ABS fails. Fine for dry painted TV panels in some cities — **not** Bhadravati kitchen/wardrobe. |
| **Exterior MDF Grade 1** | MUF | Closer to HDWR but usually **lower density** than HDHMR. Still not BWP. Do not substitute “exterior MDF” for Boilo on kitchen doors. |
| **Particle board / prelam PB** (IS 3087 / 12823) | UF | Worst screw holding; hinge cups tear; water = crumbling. Standard cheap modular carcass. **Reject** for shutters and wardrobe leaves. |
| **Blockboard** (IS 1659) | Varies | Battens can telegraph; not for 0.8 mm Idria faces. Loft shelves only if BWP and not a show face. |

## 6. Laminate + ABS compatibility (all cores)

Locked finishes: kitchen **S1241 MT Latte 0.8 mm** + **2 mm E3 ABS**; wardrobe **84689 Idria Oak 0.8 mm** + **1 mm E3 ABS**.

| Core | 0.8 mm laminate | ABS | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calibrated composed-core ply (Club Prime, Architect, Greenply 710 Marine) | Excellent | Excellent | Balance both faces on kitchen shutters. |
| Economy BWP ply (Sainik, Ecotec, Vista 710) | Fair | Fair | Core gaps / thickness variation ? telegraphing, hinge misalignment. |
| HDHMR / HDWR | Excellent | Excellent | Factory-smooth. **Must** seal every edge; PUR if the machine is proven, else quality PVA + ABS. |
| Boilo HDF | Excellent | Excellent | Dense; sharp bits; more weight on hardware. |
| MDF / PB | MDF paints well; PB prelam is a different system | Weak | Reject. |

## 10. Specification recommendation (not Sainik-default)

**Kitchen shutters (Latte 0.8 + 2 mm ABS, 18/19 mm):**

1. **Action Tesa Boilo 18 mm** if the fabricator can edge HDF; **or**  
2. **Century Club Prime 19 mm** (IS 710 stamp + QR); **or**  
3. **Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm** (IS 710 stamp).  

Fallback: Classic Marine / Bond 710 **only with IS 710 on the edge**. **Not** Sainik, Ecotec, Vista 710, MR, MDF, PB.

**Wardrobe leaves (Idria 0.8 + 1 mm ABS, ~90 in):**

1. **Club Prime 19 mm** or **Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm** if sheet count is acceptable; **or**  
2. **Action Tesa HDHMR 18 mm** or **Greenpanel HDWR 18 mm** in a CNC shop; **or**  
3. **Classic Marine 19 mm** with IS 710 stamp.  

**Sainik 710 19 mm BWP** is a **budget contingency**, not the written default. Boilo is **too heavy** for these tall doors.

**If boxes are ever added under the sink:** plywood **IS 710** (Club Prime / Greenply Marine), not HDHMR/Boilo as the wet carcass.
