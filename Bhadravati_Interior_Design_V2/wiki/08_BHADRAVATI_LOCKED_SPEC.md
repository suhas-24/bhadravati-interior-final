## 0. Eight-line lock (read this first)

1. **Kitchen = shutters only** on existing **black granite**. No new carcass, island, or dual-tone. All faces: Century **S1241 MT Latte** 0.8 mm (live page 2026-08-12). **2 mm E3 ABS**. Recessed brushed SS pulls.
2. **Kitchen core:** **Century Club Prime 19 mm** with **IS 710 on the sheet-edge stamp** + CenturyPromise QR. **Peer:** Greenply **710 Marine 19 mm** (IS 710 stamp). **CNC alt:** Action Tesa **Boilo 18 mm** if the shop already edges HDF. **Not** Sainik / Ecotec / Vista / Lincoln as default.
3. **Wardrobe = 3 hinged leaves** **457 / 457 / 458 mm** inside clear **1372 × 2286 × 488 mm** (54 × 90 × 19.20 in). Face: Century **84689 SU Idria Oak** — Woodgrains **European Grey** (not taupe). Screen approx `#3D483C`. **1 mm E3 ABS**.
4. **Wardrobe core:** Club Prime **19 mm IS 710 stamp** **or** Greenply **710 Marine 19 mm**. **CNC:** HDHMR / HDWR **18 mm** (IS 12406 Gr.1), full seal. **Boilo too heavy** for 90 in doors (~20–22 kg/leaf). **Sainik 710 19 mm = contingency only.**
5. **Hardware:** **Hettich Sensys 8645i** 110° integrated soft-close — **5 cups per 90 in leaf**. **Not Onsys.** Channels **KA 5632 / KA 4732** if drawers. **Godrej Curvo 8010 is a furniture lock, not a hinge.** Oval **30×15 mm** rail + **mid-support**. **SS304** wet-risk. **Recessed SS only — no bar pulls.**
6. **Paint:** Birla Opus **NN9074** Puddle of Grey `#B5AB9C` default; ceiling **WW0005**. Lighting **3000 K**.
7. **TV cabinet only:** **80236 DW Slate Grey** (not kitchen; not 80236 SU).
8. **Reject:** MR, interior MDF, particle board, Uniply (liquidation), Mayur as specified brand, unbranded “710”, 16 mm tall doors, gloss/sparkle, projecting bars.

## 2. Boards — first principles applied (`01` `02` `03` `06`)

| Location | Primary (write this) | Alternate | Contingency | Forbidden default |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kitchen shutters | **Club Prime 19 mm**, **IS 710 edge stamp**, QR, laminate **both faces** | **Boilo 18 mm** CNC; **Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm** IS 710 | Classic Marine / Bond 710 / Austin Marine / Kitply Gold / Apple Platinum Plus+ **only if IS 710 on edge** | Sainik, Ecotec 710, Vista 710, Lincoln 710, Uniply, Mayur, MR, MDF, PB |
| Kitchen loft | Same ply as shutters **or** 18 mm HDHMR **if fully ABS-sealed** | — | — | Open-edge fibre |
| Wardrobe carcass + 3 leaves | **Club Prime 19 mm IS 710** **or** **Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm** | **HDHMR / HDWR 18 mm** CNC | **Sainik 710 19 mm IS 303 BWP** + QR + cut-test + 5 hinges | **Boilo** (mass); Architect as whole-niche; blockboard doors |

**Why Club Prime / 710 Marine, not Sainik (`01` `06`):** Sainik and Ecotec **print IS 303 BWP**. The trademark **“710” is not a grade**. Club Prime’s website says “marine-grade” **without printing IS 710** — **accept only if the edge stamp is IS 710**. Greenply **710 Marine** is the SKU that **prints IS 710**. QR ? marine. Quora cluster: Sainik/Ecotec **outsourced / alternate-core** — inspect the cut; do not make that the kitchen default.

**Why not Boilo on wardrobe (`03`):** Boilo is **HDF >1000 kg/m³**, not ply, not marine, outside IS 12406’s 600–900 band. Independent **2–5 year diaries do not exist** (`06`). Extra **5–9 kg per 90 in leaf** is a sag tax. Use Boilo on **kitchen shutters in a CNC shop** only.

**HDHMR** is a **trade name** on **IS 12406 Grade 1**. Moisture resistance is **resin + wax + edge seal**. Open edges = elephant swell (`03` `06`).

## 6. Fabrication hold-points

- Shop-made shutters, site hang. Granite niche is never square — scribe; do not plane ABS off.
- Kitchen: both faces + 2 mm ABS full perimeter + seal hinge cups.
- Wardrobe: 1 mm ABS all edges including shelves and rod holes; **5 Sensys**; oval rail mid-support.
- Sample **one kitchen door** (raking 3000 K, no bubbles, recessed pull wrapped) before the full run.
- Spares: 1 laminate sheet each code, 10–15 m ABS each, 2 pulls, 1 Sensys pair, 1 Curvo, 1 SL 322.
