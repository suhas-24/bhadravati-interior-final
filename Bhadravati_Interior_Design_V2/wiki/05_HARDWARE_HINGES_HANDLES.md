## 3. Hinges — honest review, named SKUs

### 3.1 What “good” means on this job

Daily kitchen cycles + **90 in** wardrobe mass (19 mm + 0.8 mm Idria + 1 mm ABS). Failure is **cup tear-out**, **sag at the latch edge**, and **dead dampers** that then slam and finish the tear-out.

Rules:

1. **Integrated** soft-close in the hinge (Sensys Silent System / Blum BLUMOTION / Metalla with soft-close **in the SKU name**). Not a clip-on damper on a cheap cup. Designer commentary (Studio Matrx, cited in the plywood/hardware note): clip-on dampers often die in **~18–24 months** — anecdote, matches trade habit.
2. **3D adjustment** (overlay / depth / height at plate) because granite niches are not square.
3. **Enough hinges.** Two or three cups on a 90 in door is how leaves twist. Use **five**.
4. **Same family on one leaf.** Do not mix Sensys arms with Blum plates.

### 3.2 Hettich Sensys 8645i — **primary**

**Official (global shop / brochure):** concealed clip-on; **integrated Silent System**; **EN 15570 Level 3**; door **15–24 mm**; cup **Ø 35 mm**, depth **12.8 mm**; overlay ±2 mm; depth +3/?2 mm; height at plate; nickel-plated steel cup and arm.

| Variant you must name | Typical order no. (global) | Use here |
|---|---|---|
| Sensys 8645i 110° **overlay**, TH 52 × 5.5, **screw-on** | **9073605** (50) / **9071205** (200) | Default kitchen + wardrobe **outer** stiles if overlay on hanging strip |
| Sensys 8645i 110° **half overlay** | Confirm India pack; same 8645i family | **Meeting stiles** of the three wardrobe leaves |
| Sensys 8645i 110° **inset** | e.g. **9073616** (Fix / inset — **not** the default unless shop drawings say inset) | Only if granite/frame forces inset |
| Mounting plates | **System 8000** (height-adjustable), overlay distance to match crank | One plate per hinge; same brand |

**India invoice must say `Sensys 8645i`**, not “Hettich hinge 110°”. Hettich India also sells **Onsys** (value line). Woodage’s 2026 comparison treats Onsys as the budget Hettich and Sensys as the mid-tier everyday soft-close — **practice blog, not a lab report**. **Do not accept Onsys** on kitchen or 90 in doors.

**Honest review:** Best **value-reliable** default in India: Hyderabad-era supply, spare cups exist outside metros, Silent System is in the cup. Not as “buttery” as Blum in showroom demos (Quora / installer blogs). Fake risk is **high** (see §10). Nickel plating is not SS304; still the correct European cup for this job.

### 3.5 Ebco — wardrobe-only value cut, not kitchen

Ebco **Auto / Auto Plus** 110° overlay with **integrated** (not clip-on) damper is the honest Indian mid-market cup. Team-BHP: users report **Ebco + Hettich mix**, “90% at 50% price,” warranty easier on Hettich when bills were kept ([p.148](https://www.team-bhp.com/forum/shifting-gears/100713-home-construction-makeover-maintenance-thread-148.html)).

**Use:** optional on **internal** wardrobe shelves/loft if Sensys count blows the budget — **not** on kitchen shutters, **not** as the only hinge on 90 in leaves.

**Do not use:** nameless “Ebco-type” cups; damper clips; 2-hinge tall doors.

### 3.6 Local / unbranded / “SS hinge”

Local 110° cups are the usual carpenter default. They look like Sensys in a photo. They are **not** EN 15570 Level 3 because nobody tested *that* box. “SS” in the bazaar is often **201** or plated MS. **Reject** for kitchen and 90 in wardrobe.

### 3.7 Hinge count and overlay (this geometry)

**Wardrobe (locked 3-door, 90 in):**

| Leaf | Width (lock) | Hinges | Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left | 457 mm | **5** Sensys 8645i | Overlay at wall stile; **half overlay** at meeting stile |
| Centre | 457 mm | **5** | Half overlay both meeting stiles (or overlay + rebate per shop drawing) |
| Right | 458 mm | **5** | Overlay at wall stile; half overlay at meeting stile |

**Opening angle:** **110°** is enough for 488 mm depth. **165°** needs side clearance the granite run may not have. Do not “upgrade” angle without a swing drawing.

## 7. Screws — SS304 vs MS (and what the hinge box already has)

| Use | Spec | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen wet-risk, plinth, hanging strip into humid zone | **SS304** wood screws / confirmat (A2). Magnet test is **not** proof (304 is weakly magnetic when cold-worked). Demand **304 stamped** packets + bill. | MS zinc dies when plating chips; Bhadravati monsoon + sink steam. Project lock: SS304 wet-risk (`SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`). |
| Hinge **cups and plates** | **Screws in the branded box** (Hettich ~3.5 × 15–17 mm; Blum **609.1500 / 609.1700**). Predrill ~2.5 mm. | Random 1-inch MS screws **cam-out** and split cups. |
| Wardrobe carcass (dry interior) | Confirmat **7×50** or **6.3×50** into 19 mm; zinc OK **inside**; SS304 still preferred at **plinth**. | |
| Hanging-rod brackets | **4 × 30 mm** into 19 mm; SS304 or nickel-plated. | |
| Granite / masonry | **Do not** use 15 mm wood screws into stone. Hanging **strip** first. | #1 kitchen retrofit failure. |

**SS201 vs 304:** 201 is cheaper “stainless” that rusts faster in coastal/monsoon kitchens. If the packet does not say **304**, treat as suspect.

**MS (mild steel) zinc:** acceptable **inside** a dry wardrobe box. **Not** at the kitchen hanging strip, sink-adjacent, or rod brackets that see wet clothes.

Team-BHP (MDF/PVC discussion): screw **creep** in fibre boards — use **correct confirmat / dowel**, not random wood screws ([p.64](https://www.team-bhp.com/forum/shifting-gears/100713-home-construction-makeover-maintenance-thread-64.html)). Same logic if wardrobe goes **HDHMR**.

## 8. Handles vs recessed G-profile / J-profile — **LOCKED recessed SS**

### 8.1 Client lock (do not reopen)

**Brushed stainless recessed** pulls — J-pull / finger-pull / recessed profile. **No projecting bar handles** (`CONTRADICTIONS.md` C-08; `MASTER_BRIEF_V2.md`; tokens `handle_type: recessed`).

Reasons that still hold:

- Studio circulation: bars catch hips, towels, and laterite dust.
- 3-door 90 in: bars on three tall leaves look busy on Idria; recessed keeps the woodgrain.
- Kitchen wipe-down: fewer projecting crevices.
- Safety: lever/recessed preferred over bars in the brief.

**Do not “upgrade” to bar pulls** because a carpenter has them in stock or an elevation PNG still shows bars. Captions override drawings (`FINAL_QA_CHECKLIST.md` B2).

### 8.2 What “recessed” may be (all still non-projecting)

| Type | What it is | This job |
|---|---|---|
| **Recessed SS finger-pull / edge pull** | Short brushed-SS cup or edge extrusion milled into the shutter | **Primary.** Match **2 mm** kitchen ABS so the mill does not chip S1241. Wardrobe: same family, slimmer for **1 mm** ABS. |
| **J-profile** | J-shaped extrusion on the **vertical** or top edge; fingers hook the J | **Allowed** if finish is **brushed SS / stainless-look**. Named examples: Häfele **Profin under-counter J Gola** ([hafeleindia.com P-01459079](https://www.hafeleindia.com/en/product/profin-under-counter-j-gola-profile-length-3000-mm-useable-length-2800mm/P-01459079/)); Ozone **OEAP-J 3M AN** (silver anodised, 18/19 mm panels). |
| **G-profile / Gola (C / L / G)** | Aluminium handleless channel: **L** under counter, **C** between drawers, **G/Gola** wall/tall | **Allowed only** as handleless **recessed** language, finish **brushed SS look**, not gold/black fashion unless client re-locks. Hettich Gola 3000 mm (retailer listings) — **confirm Hettich India article** on the invoice. Vertical C/L Gola can serve **3-door** meeting edges. |
| **Bar / D / knob** | Projects 20–40 mm | **Forbidden** on this package. |

**G-profile vs J-profile (practical):**

- **J** = one-sided hook; good on **wardrobe leaf edges** and loft underside.
- **G / C Gola** = channel in the **carcass** or between fronts; more factory-modular. On a **site-built granite kitchen**, a **milled J / recessed SS pull on the shutter** is usually more reliable than a full Gola carcass system you are not rebuilding.

**Do not** mix gold Gola with brushed SS elsewhere. **Do not** use gloss chrome bars “just on the kitchen.”

### 8.3 3-door wardrobe pull layout

One **vertical recessed** pull per leaf, aligned at a common height (~900–1100 mm AFF to grip centre — confirm reach). Centre leaf still recessed — no dummy bar. Lock cylinder (Curvo) sits **separate** from the pull so the dimple key does not fight the J.

## 9. Per-item purchase checklist (box hologram, bill, spare count)

**Global rules for every line:** authorised dealer; **GST tax invoice** with **brand + series + article/SKU + qty + finish**; photograph **unopened box** (hologram/QR) **and** one opened sample (logo **moulded/etched on the metal**, not a peel sticker); keep empty boxes until handover; do not accept “loose hinges in a polybag.”

## 10. Fake-risk (India) — what actually happens

| Pattern | What to do |
|---|---|
| Quote says **“Hettich”** once for the whole house | Demand **counted BOQ**: Sensys 8645i × N, KA 4732 × length × N, plates × N. Woodage: mixing genuine visible hinges with local runners is a known dodge. |
| **Onsys** or Intermat sold as Sensys | Read the **arm**. Onsys is the value line. |
| **Loose** hinges, no box | Treat as fake/grey. Warranty is on **invoice + packaging**. |
| Hologram missing / peeling logo sticker | Homeli/Trinabh practice: laser/moulded logo + box hologram. Walk away. |
| Price “too good” | Installer blogs cite Sensys in a few-hundred-rupees-each band at trade — **not a live quote**. If it is half of every other dealer, it is the wrong line or fake. |
| **Godrej hinges** | Naming fraud / ignorance. Specify Curvo **lock** separately and Sensys **hinges** separately. |
| Blum boxes, local cups inside | Open **random** boxes at delivery, not the sample the vendor prepared. |
| Chrome rod with no brand | Fine if **30×15 oval + mid support**; still prefer Hettich/Ebco for brackets. Fake risk is lower than hinges; **sag risk** is higher. |
