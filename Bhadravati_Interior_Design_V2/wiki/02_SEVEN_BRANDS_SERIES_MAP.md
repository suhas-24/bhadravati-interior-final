# Multi-brand plywood cartographer — named series map

**Project use:** Bhadravati kitchen **shutters-only** (steam, 0.8 mm Century S1241 Latte, 2 mm ABS, daily hinge cups) vs **~90 in** three-leaf wardrobe (monsoon humidity, termites, 0.8 mm Idria Oak, 1 mm ABS, 5 cups/leaf).  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**This page owns:** Kitply, Austin Ply, Century **Classic Marine / Bond 710**, Uniply, Mayur / National (NPI), plus Karnataka-stocked regional lines.  
**Sibling owns depth:** CenturyPly Club Prime / Architect / Sainik and Greenply 710 Marine / Club / Gold / Platinum — **cross-ref only** here. Spec lock remains `BOARD_DECISION.md`.

Manufacturer pages = **claims**. Dealer blogs = **habit + availability**. A product **name** containing “710” is **not** an IS 710 stamp.

---

## 0. How to read grades (stamp, not slogan)

| Spoken name | What BIS actually is | Kitchen shutters | 90 in wardrobe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MR** | IS 303 MR — typically UF / MUF. Humidity, not wetting. | **Reject** | **Reject** in Bhadravati (plinth mop + monsoon). Dry loft only if already sealed. |
| **BWR** | IS 303 BWR — boiling-water *resistant*. Not marine. | Loft only if BWP/710 unavailable | Acceptable carcass if BWP unavailable; not first choice |
| **BWP as IS 303 BWP** | General-purpose boiling-waterproof. Century **Sainik 710** official disclaimer is this. | Not first choice | Contingency if IS 710 sheet not on the truck |
| **Marine / IS 710** | IS 710:2024 marine plywood; IS 848 **BWP** adhesive; wet bending + preservative retention. | **Demand this stamp** on ply path | Prefer this stamp |
| **“710” in the SKU name** | Marketing. Sainik 710, Vista 710, Ecotec 710, Lincoln 710, Bond 710. | Photograph **ISI + IS number + CML** on the **edge** | Same |
| **IS 10701** | Structural plywood (Club 700, Austin Club Plus). Strength, not automatically marine. | Only if **also** IS 710 / 303 BWP on the edge | OK if dense + calibrated; still seal edges |
| **IS 5509** | Fire-retardant plywood. | Nice near hob; not a kitchen-moisture substitute | Overkill |
| **BS 1088 / Lloyd’s** | UK marine (Austin Platinum Plus claim). | Luxury if stocked; verify Indian ISI too | Overkill for a bedroom niche |

**IS 710 product manual (BIS):** [PM/IS 710 July 2024](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-IS-710-_July-24.pdf) — adhesive must be BWP type of IS 848; licence scope includes thickness + formaldehyde class.

## 10. Purchase checklists

### 10.1 Every sheet (kitchen or wardrobe)

1. **Authorised dealer invoice** with mill, **series name**, thickness, grade, number of sheets. Cash-only “same brand” is a fake vector.
2. Photograph **before unloading**: face brand, **edge ISI mark**, **IS number** (303 vs 710 vs 10701 vs 5509), **CML number**, thickness, QR if CenturyPromise / Greenply warranty app.
3. **Mic thickness** in three places. Kitchen and 90 in doors: **19 mm** (18 mm only if the mill’s nominal is 18 and cups still fit). Reject 16 mm doors.
4. **Cut-test** one offcut: core gaps, overlaps, voids at the hinge-cup zone. 0.8 mm laminate telegraphs junk cores.
5. Register warranty **the same day** (CenturyPromise / Greenply app / Apple dealer form). Unregistered “lifetime” is a poster.
6. **Same mill + same thickness** for a room. Do not mix Sainik carcass with Club Prime doors without mics.
7. Edges: kitchen **2 mm E3 ABS** full perimeter; wardrobe **1 mm**. Raw PF edges fail in Bhadravati monsoon regardless of mill.
8. Termite: board GLP ? building treatment. Site treatment still required.

### 10.2 Kitchen shutters extra

- Steam + wipe-down + daily 35 mm cups ? **IS 710 ply** or **Boilo HDF** (CNC path in `BOARD_DECISION.md`), not IS 303 BWP named “710”.
- Laminate **both faces** (S1241 + balancer).
- Reject: MR, BWR, Vista 710, Sainik default, Ecotec 710, Lincoln 710, Uniply, Mayur, Hunsur BWR, particle board, interior MDF.

### 10.3 90 in wardrobe extra

- Five integrated soft-close hinges per leaf; oval hanging rail + mid support (hardware lock unchanged).
- Prefer **IS 710** or **IS 303 BWP** composed/calibrated 19 mm, or **HDHMR 18 mm** in a CNC shop.
- **Reject Boilo** on 90 in doors (mass). **Reject blockboard** doors. **Reject Architect / Platinum Plus as the whole niche** unless leftover from kitchen (cost).
- If only Sainik / Lincoln / National Gold arrives: 19 mm, QR/ISI, cut-test, **contingency**, not the written default.

### 10.4 Fake-risk ranking (this geography)

**Highest:** Uniply leftovers · generic “710” stickers · Kitply Vista 710 · Sainik 710 grey · Mayur Gold/Ultra · Bond 710 without mill page · Lincoln 710.  
**Medium:** Classic Marine · Kitply Marine/Plus · Austin Gold · NPI Gold/Classic · Woodland labels.  
**Lower:** Club Prime / Architect QR at authorised Century · Greenply 710 Marine authorised · Apple Platinum Plus+ authorised · Austin Platinum Plus with both Lloyd’s **and** ISI.

### 10.5 Bhadravati / Shimoga yard script (one paragraph)

> Kitchen shutters: **Century Club Prime 19 mm**, edge stamp **IS 710**, CenturyPromise QR, or **Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm** same stamp. Alternate ply: **Austin Marine / Austin Gold**, **Kitply Gold / Kitply Marine**, **Apple Platinum Plus+**, **NPI Platinum Club** — only with **IS 710** on the edge. Not Sainik, not Vista 710, not Ecotec, not Uniply. Wardrobe: same Club Prime or Greenply 710 Marine; Apple **Club Plus+ Gold** (IS 303 BWP) acceptable; Sainik 710 only if the specified SKU is not on the truck.

## 12. Assumptions

- Street prices move; bands are relative to Century published MRP, not a bid.
- E0 / “zero formaldehyde” / 200-hour boil / 7× money-back are **mill marketing** until a **batch certificate** is in the file.
- Availability in Bhadravati is worse than Bengaluru; a specified SKU that is not on the truck will be substituted — the checklists exist to catch that.
- This page is a **cartographer**, not a BIS certificate and not a change to `BOARD_DECISION.md`.
