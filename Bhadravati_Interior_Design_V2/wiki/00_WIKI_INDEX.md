## What this wiki is

Indian joinery language is a trap. Product names contain **“710”** that are **not** IS 710. “Marine,” “BWP,” and “waterproof” are used interchangeably in yards. Forum praise is mixed with dealer SEO. This wiki separates:

| Layer | Question it answers | Article |
|---|---|---|
| **Standards** | What the **edge stamp** must say (IS 303 vs IS 710 vs IS 12406). | `01`, `03` |
| **Mills / series** | Century, Greenply, Action Tesa, Kitply, Austin, Uniply, Mayur, Apple. Official page vs retailer. | `01`, `02` |
| **Surfacing** | Finish letters are part of the SKU. E3 ABS. PUR vs PVA. Both-faces rule. | `04` |
| **Hardware** | Named SKUs. Godrej Curvo is a **lock**, not a hinge. Fakes. | `05` |
| **Field reviews** | Repeated failure modes — **not** a brand ranking. Reddit 403. | `06` |
| **This house** | What to write on the **BOQ** and tick at the yard. | `07`, `08` |

## First principles (do not invert)

1. **Bhadravati monsoon + termites.** Failures in the South-India record (`06`) are open edges, MR in wet zones, cheap post-form, untreated sites, and **fakes** — not “you didn’t buy Architect.”
2. **Kitchen is a small shutter count on granite.** Spend on **IS 710 stamp** (or proven HDF edging) + 2 mm ABS + daily-cycle hinges. Do not rebuild a wet carcass unless the site proves boxes are rotten.
3. **Wardrobe is three ~90 in hinged leaves.** Controlling risk is hinge tear-out, sag, and 0.8 mm Idria telegraph — not rupees per square foot on Sainik.
4. **Name ? stamp.** Photograph **ISI + IS number + CML** on every sheet. Century: **QR before unload**. QR proves sticker authenticity, **not** marine grade and **not** a core-gap X-ray (`01`, `06`).
5. **Availability is not a performance argument.** Sainik is what the truck often has. That does not make it the BOQ default.
6. **Year 3–7 cost is rework**, not skipping Club Prime. Factory **edge-bander** beats iron-on (`04`, `06`).
7. **Website ?/sqft is often 6 mm.** Compare **landed 19 mm** quotes (`01`).

Decision tree: carpenter / hand tools ? Club Prime 19 mm **IS 710 stamp** (Greenply 710 Marine peer). CNC + proven HDF edging ? Boilo 18 mm **kitchen only**. Wardrobe CNC ? HDHMR/HDWR 18 mm, **not** Boilo. Sainik only if specified SKUs cannot arrive in 7–14 days.
