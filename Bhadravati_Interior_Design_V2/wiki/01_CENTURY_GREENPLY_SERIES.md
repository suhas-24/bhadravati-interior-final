# CenturyPly + Greenply series — technical truth (not brochure)

**Role:** Plywood brand-series expert (Century + Greenply).  
**Job context:** Bhadravati, Karnataka — first-floor studio; kitchen shutters on existing granite; wardrobe niche. Climate: inland monsoon humidity + termites + kitchen steam. Not a boat, not a coastal hull.  
**Date:** 2026-08-12.  
**Rule:** Official product **page** text and **BIS product manuals** outrank blogs, dealer flyers, and Instagram. Marketing is labelled **[MARKETING]**. Unpublished numbers are **not invented**.  
**Status:** Research note for specification. **Not a BIS certificate. Not a bid. Not a warranty.**

Related project files: `BOARD_DECISION.md`, `BOARD_OPTIONS_CATALOG.md`, `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`.

---

## How to read this file

1. **Stamp on the sheet** (ISI mark + IS number + CML) is the only legal grade. Product names containing “710”, “Marine”, “Club”, or “BWP” are not grades.
2. **IS 303:2024** and **IS 710:2024** both exist. Since 2024, **BWP is a grade inside IS 303** (general-purpose plywood). **IS 710** is a **different standard**: marine plywood for craft / high-moisture construction. They are not synonyms.
3. Brand websites contradict their own blogs. This file records **what the product page prints today**, then flags older blog claims.
4. Prices: Century “?/unit” where **1 unit ? 929 cm² ? 1 sq ft**. Greenply website ?/sqft is often for the **default thickness on that page (frequently 6 mm)** and **exclusive of tax**. Do not treat 6 mm website stickers as 19 mm Karnataka kitchen rates.
5. Screw-holding figures are **manufacturer published**, not BIS-mandated pass/fail numbers. They are not a waterproof test.

## 1. BIS map (believe this first)

### 1.1 Current standards (2024 revisions)

| Standard | Official title | What it actually covers | Primary source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IS 303:2024** | Plywood for General Purposes — Specification (Fourth Revision) | **Grades: MR, BWR, and BWP.** Appearance types AA/AB/BB. Formaldehyde E1/E2. Furniture / interior general-purpose ply. | [BIS product manual PM/IS 303/3/July 2024](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-IS-303-_July-24.pdf) |
| **IS 710:2024** | Marine Plywood — Specification (Third Revision; supersedes IS 710:2010) | Plywood for **construction, repair and maintenance of marine and river craft, pontoons and the like**. Adhesive must be **BWP type of IS 848**. Extra tests vs general-purpose: water resistance, **wet bending**, tensile, **mycological**, **preservative retention**, formaldehyde. | [BIS product manual PM/IS 710/3/July 2024](https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/PM-IS-710-_July-24.pdf); [Intertek Inform abstract](https://inus04aapb1h3nprod.dxcloud.episerver.net/en-au/standards/is-710-2024-184961_saig_bis_bis_3396955/) |
| **IS 848** | Synthetic resin adhesives for plywood | Defines **BWP / BWR / MR adhesive types**. IS 710 adhesive clause: BWP type; **extender shall not be added** by the plywood manufacturer; fillers if used **? 10%** by mass of glue solids (text of IS 710:2024 as circulated). | Cited inside IS 710 manual + IS 710:2024 body |
| **IS 10701:2012** | Structural plywood — Specification | Load-bearing / engineering plywood (stressed-skin, web beams, sheathing, containers). Adhesive: **hot-press un-extended PF, BWP type of IS 848**. Not “marine” by name. | [law.resource.org IS 10701:2012](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.10701.2012.pdf); [BIS plywood compendium](https://services.bis.gov.in/tmp/compendium_2025-06-02-04-13-12.pdf) |
| **IS 5509** | Fire-retardant plywood | Fire performance. A sheet can be FR **and** still be IS 303 or IS 710 or neither. **IS 5509 ? waterproof.** | BIS (see also Century Architect / Greenply Platinum pages) |
| **IS 1659** | Blockboards | Different product. Do not specify “plywood” and accept blockboard. | BIS |
| **IS 12406** | MDF | HDHMR / HDF family lives here, **not** under IS 710. | BIS |
| **IS 5539 / IS 401** | Preservative treatment of plywood / timber | Brand “anti-termite” claims often point here. Treatment ? marine grade. | Cited on Greenply product pages |

**IS 710:2024 listed thicknesses (manual):** 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 12, **15**, **19**, 22, 25 mm. **16 mm and 18 mm are not in that list**; they are “other thickness as agreed.” Yards still sell 16/18/19. **Mic the sheet.**

**IS 303:2024 licence scope (manual) must declare:** grade (MR/BWR/BWP), appearance type, thickness, formaldehyde class, **type of synthetic resin, usage of extenders, type of preservative treatment**. That last trio is why “BWP” on an IS 303 sheet is **not automatically** an IS 710 marine sheet.

### 1.2 Why BIS added BWP to IS 303 (industry, not marketing)

Trade reporting (not a BIS press release): consumers were buying “710 BWP” for interiors because they could not tell **IS 710** from the **number 710** in a brand name; BIS added a **BWP grade under IS 303** so general-purpose furniture ply could be boiling-waterproof **without** being marine-craft plywood. Century’s own technical advisor is quoted in that coverage.

- Secondary: [AMC / industry note on BWP added to IS 303](https://amcvietnam.com.vn/en/bwp-is-added-to-the-is-303-standard-on-plywood/)
- Secondary: [Ply Insight on IS 303:2024 including BWP](https://plyinsight.com/mastering-is-303-strategic-insights-into-plywood-testing-standards-and-industry-impact-part-3/)

**Design judgment (labelled):** For a Bhadravati **kitchen wet zone**, specify **IS 710 stamped on the edge** because of wet bending + mycological + preservative retention — not because the house is a boat. For a **dry wardrobe**, **IS 303 BWP** can be a legitimate cheaper path **if** the stamp, core, calibration, and QR check out. Do not let a carpenter equate the two.

### 1.3 Tests that actually differ (high level)

| Topic | IS 303:2024 general-purpose (incl. BWP grade) | IS 710:2024 marine |
|---|---|---|
| Intended use (standard’s own words) | General purpose | Marine / river craft, pontoons **and the like** |
| Adhesive | Manufacturer **declares** resin type and **whether extenders are used** | **BWP type of IS 848**; **no extender** added by ply mill; fillers capped |
| Water resistance | Required (clause 11.3.1 in the 303 manual) | Required (clause 9.5) **every control unit** |
| Mycological | Monthly sample (303 manual Table 1) | Monthly sample (710 manual) |
| Wet bending / tensile / preservative retention | **Not** in the 303 manual’s routine table the way 710 lists them | **Explicit** clauses 9.6, 9.8, 9.9, 9.10 |
| Marking | ISI + **IS 303** + **grade MR/BWR/BWP** | ISI + **IS 710** + “Marine Plywood” |

**[MARKETING] “72-hour boil”** is a popular shorthand. Confirm the **edition and clause** on the mill test certificate. Do not accept a verbal “we boil it for 72 hours” as IS 710.

## 5. Karnataka / Bhadravati monsoon — what actually matters

**Facts**

- Bhadravati is **inland Karnataka**, not a shipyard. IS 710’s **scope sentence** is marine craft; Indian interiors use it as the **strictest moisture + fungus + preservative** plywood spec.
- Failure modes that show up in South-India kitchens: **open edges**, **MR in wet zones**, **counterfeit sheets**, **gappy cores under 0.8 mm laminate**, **hinge tear-out**, **termite at untreated cut edges** — not “you didn’t buy Architect.”
- Monsoon humidity + kitchen steam is **cyclic wetting**, not 72-hour continuous boil. Boil tests are a **glue-line proxy**, not a simulation of a Bhadravati August.

**Design judgment**

- **Kitchen shutters (this job):** write **Club Prime 19 mm or Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm**, **IS 710 on the edge**, CenturyPromise or Greenply registration, laminate both faces, **2 mm ABS**, SS304 screws. See `BOARD_DECISION.md`.
- **Wardrobe:** same mill if the carpenter is ply-native; **IS 303 BWP** is a **minimum**, not a target. HDHMR 18 mm is a **CNC** path, not a carpenter-with-hand-tools path.
- **Do not** specify 16 mm for doors. **Do not** mix 18 mm MR/HDHMR with 19 mm ply in one elevation without a hardware drawing.
- Edge seal and hardware quality will outrun a one-step brand upgrade.

## 7. Universal pre-purchase checklist (every series)

Copy this onto the BOQ cover:

1. **Name is not the grade.** Photograph **ISI + IS 303 or IS 710 or IS 10701 or IS 5509 + grade + CML**.
2. Verify CML on BIS. Verify QR/warranty app.
3. **Mic** thickness in three places; reject > tolerance bounce (0.8 mm laminate telegraphs).
4. Cut a 50 mm strip: voids, overlaps, paper cores, fungal stain, dry glue.
5. Match **16 vs 18 vs 19** to hardware (Sensys cups, carcass connectors).
6. **PF / BWP resin** on mill cert if wet zone; MR UF does not become BWP because someone boiled a sample in a bucket.
7. Register warranty **the same day**; read exclusions (standing water, unselaed edges, outdoor).
8. Authorised dealer invoice with **series name + thickness + IS number**.
9. Do not accept blockboard, particle board, or interior MDF as “the same 19 mm.”
10. For this house: **kitchen ? Sainik/Ecotec default.**
