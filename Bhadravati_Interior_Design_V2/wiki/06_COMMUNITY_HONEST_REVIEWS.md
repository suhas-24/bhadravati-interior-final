## 10. Field consensus (community only)

1. **Fakes (ply + hinges) are the Indian special** — scan ply, keep hardware cartons.  
2. **Grade + sealed edges + thickness beat brand slogans.** MR at the sink and open edges show up by year 3–5.  
3. **Sainik is economy Century**, not Club/marine-IS-710 by default.  
4. **HDHMR/Boilo have almost no independent 2–5 year homeowner writing**; MDF/particle have plenty of 2–5 year obituaries.  
5. **Laminate peel = edge + heat + adhesive + site tape**, not the décor name.  
6. **ABS vs PVC is underspecified in forums**; Rehau + 2 mm + machine is what people actually name.  
7. **Hettich is the replacement brand** in Team-BHP; replicas at ~60% price are openly sold; install skill still kills soft-close.  
8. **Monsoon/termite:** treat the building; don’t trust “termite-proof” stickers; post-form + cheap core is the South-India casualty list.  
9. **Factory vs carpenter:** machine edges vs babysitting. Neither channel is honest if the quote only says “Hettich” and “marine.”
