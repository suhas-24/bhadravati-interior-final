## 2. Century ladder: Sainik 710 vs Club vs Architect vs Marine

### 2.1 The naming trap (do not take marketing as the spec)

Centuryùs **own product page** for Sainik 710 states that ùAsliù BWP means **IS 303 BWP (general-purpose plywood)** ù not automatically **IS 710 marine**:

- https://www.centuryply.com/plywood/sainik-710  
- Same disclaimer: https://www.centuryply.com/sainik-710-2021/

Century **blogs** and **Bangalore retailers** still write ùSainik 710 = IS:710 marine.ù That is **Class C**, and it **contradicts** the product-page footnote.

Examples of the conflation (do not treat as certificates):

- https://www.centuryply.com/blog/waterproof-your-worries-5-ways-sainik-710-makes-home-interiors-stress-free (blog claims ISI IS:710)  
- https://home-run.co/blogs/homerun-knowledge-base/century-plywood-vs-greenply-archidply-bangalore (ù710 signifies IS:710ù)  
- https://plybasket.com/waterproof-plywood-brands-india-best-plywood-brands-india-marine-plywood-india-bwp-plywood-brands-waterproof-plywood-for-kitchen/

**Field implication for Bhadravati:** paying for ù710ù on the sticker is not the same as getting **IS 710 marine**. If the kitchen shutter spec needs marine phenolic, the BOQ must name **Club Prime / Architect / a stamped IS 710 SKU**, then **scan + photograph the edge stamp**.

### 2.2 Counterfeit QR ù repeated, not a Century invention

**Class A (problem is real). Class C (Centuryùs app as the only solution).**

Century launched CenturyPromise because the organised ply market is flooded with lookalikes. That is an admission of a **market failure**, not proof that genuine Sainik lasts 10 years.

- Company launch copy: https://www.commercialdesignindia.com/uncategorized/9418-centuryply-launches-century-promise-app-first-of-its-kind-initiative-to-protect-consumers-from-fake-plywood  
- Industry interview (Century on fakes): https://plyinsight.com/menace-of-fake-branding-and-its-solutions/  
- App itself: https://www.centuryply.com/centurypromise/

**Class D (Reddit via retailer):** HomeRun paraphrases `r/bangalore` ù ùThe plywood I purchased, which was supposed to be CenturyWood, was fakeù and ùThese people sell fake plywood and play with people's hard-earned money.ù No thread URL in the article.

- https://home-run.co/blogs/homerun-knowledge-base/century-plywood-vs-greenply-archidply-bangalore

**Class C caveat:** some clone-script blogs claim fake QR codes are already in the wild. Unverified. Practical check remains: **scan on the shop floor, before the sheet is cut**, with the official app, plus **BIS CML on the edge**. A passed QR is authenticity of *that millùs sticker*, not a core-gap X-ray.

**YouTube process clip (not a durability review):** an interiors firm in Beeramguda scans Sainik 710 QR with the client and generates e-warranty on the spot. That is **procurement hygiene**, not a 7-year kitchen test.

## 3. Action Tesa Boilo vs Greenpanel HDHMR vs plywood for shutters

### 3.1 What users/carpenters actually argue

**Class A ù hybrid, not a holy war.**

Repeated across Team-BHP, Indusladies, and even competing trade blogs:

1. **Indian kitchens are wet.** Particle board / standard MDF in a carcass is a 4ù5 year write-off.  
2. **HDHMR is the modular-factory shutter** (flat, CNC, no core voids).  
3. **Plywood is the on-site carpenter shutter** (forgiving of hand tools, re-screwable).  
4. **Boilo is sold as ùBWP HDF / denser than HDHMRù** for wet + fire-adjacent kitchens. Almost no independent 5-year homeowner diary.

Team-BHP (user planning kitchen, steel carcass, asking for **long-term HDHMR users** ù the question itself shows the evidence gap):

> What you need for kitchens is boiling water proof (BWP) ply or alternatively this composite called HDHMR. Basic marine ply won't work. Any long term users of this HDHMR?

- https://www.team-bhp.com/forum/shifting-gears/100713-home-construction-makeover-maintenance-thread-148.html  

Team-BHP (particle / Chinese ply):

> Particle board can not take moisture and does not last beyond 4-5 yearsù he may use imported Chinese Plywood. Problem with no-name Chinese plywood is excess formaldehyde.

- https://www.team-bhp.com/forum/shifting-gears/100713-home-construction-makeover-maintenance-thread-46.html  

Indusladies (modular kitchen material): Western particle/MDF carcass **ùnot good for Indian kitchen because our kitchens are mostly wetù** ? BWR IS 303 carcass; MR only if the kitchen is semi-modular on a concrete bed.

- https://indusladies.com/community/threads/tips-for-getting-best-modular-kitchen.260972/  

### 3.2 HDHMR ù praise and the actual complaints

**Class A (positive, 1ù5 year, not 15 year):**

hdhmr.in aggregates (AI-assisted; treat quotes as **paraphrase**): Hyderabad carpenter switched from MDF to HDHMR for kitchen shutters (heavier, holds screws better than MDF). Mumbai homeowner: 2-year HDHMR kitchen, no swell. Chennai: 5-year wardrobe, no termites. Pune contractor: **open edges still fail**. Bangalore couple: 1-year HDHMR kitchen OK except swell **where laminate chipped**.

- http://www.hdhmr.in/blog/hdhmr-board-reviews-expert-user/  

**Class A (complaints that repeat):**

- **Weight** ? more hinges, heavier feel, two-person handling.  
- **Tall doors sag** if 2 hinges / 16 mm. r/IndianDIY paraphrased: sagging HDHMR wardrobe door ? add hinges + thicker board.  
- **Screw holding < plywood** on repeated removal (Centuryùs own HDMR blog is cited against HDHMR ù ironic).  
- **Chippy edges** with dull blades.  
- **MDF sold as HDHMR** in smaller towns.  
- After laminate + ABS + labour, **not much cheaper than mid ply**.

Holzbox (factory vs carpenter ù trade, but matches Team-BHP ùcarpenters use plyù):

> On-site carpenters working with hand tools and manual edge tape often get better results with plywoodù HDHMR belongs in a factory.

- https://holzbox.in/blog/what-is-hdhmr-board-and-why-is-it-replacing-plywood-in-kitchens/  

**Class B ù Greenply HDHMR warp (contractor, ~200 sheets, kitchen/crockery shutters):** LinkedIn post by Krishnan SP: expansion/warping post-install, ùbatch-related,ù client cancelled further work. Not a homeowner 7-year story; it is a **real QC complaint**.

- https://www.linkedin.com/posts/krishnan-sp-41a60087_subject-urgent-support-request-regarding-activity-7358154167112159233-eO98

## 9. What this means for Bhadravati (opinions ? spec hygiene)

Not a recommendation rewrite of `PLYWOOD_HARDWARE_RECOMMENDATION.md`. Just what the **user/carpenter record** supports:

1. **Scan every Century sheet** in the shop. Fakes are the one story Bangalore/Karnataka sources actually share.  
2. **Do not equate Sainik 710 with marine IS 710.** Centuryùs footnote vs retailer copy is the smoking gun.  
3. **Expect Sainik/Ecotec to be alternate-core.** If hinge cups on tall wardrobe leaves matter, pay for **Club-class / calibrated / HDF** or **more hinges + 18ù19 mm**, not a sticker.  
4. **Kitchen shutters:** plywood if the fabricator is on-site; HDHMR/Boilo if CNC + full ABS. Boilo has **brochure density**, not a 7-year forum.  
5. **Hardware budget is not optional.** Team-BHP: fakes are common; Hettich is what people replace *to*. Name **Sensys (or equivalent SKU)** on the BOQ; keep cartons.  
6. **Termite:** treat the **building**. GLP ply is not a soil treatment.  
7. **Edges:** 2 mm kitchen ABS, 1 mm wardrobe, factory if possible. Site Rehau failures are documented.

## 10. Top 10 consensus findings

1. **Grade + thickness + sealed edges beat brand.** ùCentury kitchenù in MR still swells at the sink. (WoodAge, Studio Matrx, Indusladies, TBHP.)  
2. **Counterfeit ply and counterfeit hinges are the real Indian special.** QR/app + authorised invoice for ply; stamped SKU + bill for hardware. (CenturyPromise existence, HomeRun/r-bangalore hearsay, TBHP 50% fake fittings.)  
3. **Sainik 710 is economy Century, not Club/Architect.** Quora: outsourced / alternate-core. Official spec: **IS 303 BWP**, despite ù710ù in the name and retailer IS:710 copy.  
4. **Core gaps show up as hinge tear-out and spinning screws**, especially on MR and 16 mm. MouthShut holes; WoodAge year 2ù3.  
5. **HDHMR is liked for shutters when factory-edged;** carpenters still prefer ply on site. Failures: weight, 2-hinge sag, open edges, mislabelled MDF. (TBHP question, Holzbox, hdhmr.in, LinkedIn Greenply warp.)  
6. **Boilo has almost no independent 3ù7 year homeowner record.** Treat density/FR claims as **Class C**.  
7. **Greenply / Kitply / Austin:** Greenply is a peer to Century with the same grade trap; Kitply is old-carpenter value if **stamped IS 710**; Austin is South-stocked budget with **inspect-every-sheet** advice and **no** strong user diary.
