## 1. First principles (this house, not a brand ladder)

### What actually has to survive

1. **Bhadravati monsoon humidity + termites.** Failures in the South-India record are **open edges, MR in wet zones, cheap post-form, untreated sites, fakes** — not “you didn’t buy Architect.”
2. **Kitchen is shutters-only on granite.** Small sheet count. Steam, wipe-down, daily hinge cycles. Spend here. Do not rebuild a wet carcass unless the site proves boxes are rotten.
3. **Wardrobe is three ~90 in hinged leaves** plus a niche carcass. Larger sheet count, but the expensive failure is **hinge tear-out / sag / 0.8 mm telegraph**, not rupees per square foot on Sainik.
4. **0.8 mm laminate** telegraphs core gaps and thickness bounce. Calibrated / composed-core ply or homogeneous HDF/HDHMR.
5. **18–19 mm cores.** Do not cut 16 mm kitchen or tall wardrobe doors. BIS lists 15/19 mm; yards sell 18/19. Mic the sheet.
6. **Cost-effective and reliable** means **year 3–7 rework cost**, not the cheapest sticker at the Shimoga yard.

### Decision tree

```
Is the fabricator a CNC / factory-edge shop, or an on-site carpenter?

?? KITCHEN SHUTTERS (steam, daily cups, 0.8 mm Latte, 2 mm ABS)
?  ?? Carpenter / hand tools  ? Club Prime 19 mm, IS 710 stamp  (Greenply 710 Marine peer)
?  ?? CNC + proven HDF edging ? Boilo 18 mm  (density/FR = marketing; no 3–7 yr diary)
?  ?? Never                    ? Sainik / Ecotec / Vista / MR / MDF / PB as kitchen default
?
?? WARDROBE 90 in (humidity, termites, 5 cups/leaf, 0.8 mm Idria, 1 mm ABS)
   ?? Same ply mill as kitchen ? Club Prime 19 mm  OR  Greenply 710 Marine 19 mm
   ?? CNC shop                 ? HDHMR / HDWR 18 mm  (not Boilo — too heavy)
   ?? Only if the specified SKU is not on the truck ? Sainik 710 19 mm IS 303 BWP, QR + cut-test
```

### Standards (do not confuse names)

| Marking | Meaning | This job |
|---|---|---|
| **IS 303:2024** MR / BWR / **BWP** | General-purpose plywood. BWP here is **interior boiling-waterproof**, not marine craft ply. | Wardrobe *minimum* if ply. **Not** kitchen first choice. |
| **IS 710:2024** | Marine plywood; IS 848 BWP adhesive; wet bending + preservative retention. | **Kitchen ply** must show this on the **edge**. Wardrobe ply should too. |
| **“710” in a product name** | Marketing. Sainik 710 official page = **IS 303 BWP**. Greenply **Ecotec 710** = **IS 303**. Greenply **710 Marine** = **IS 710**. | Name ? stamp. Photograph ISI + IS number + CML. |
| **IS 12406** | MDF / HDHMR family. Moisture-resistant, not marine ply. | HDHMR wardrobe / loft only, fully ABS-sealed. |
| **IS 3087 / interior MDF** | Particle board / Grade 2 MDF. | **Reject.** |

## 3. Why Sainik is **not** the wardrobe pick (after the full field)

The prior note (`PLYWOOD_HARDWARE_RECOMMENDATION.md`) chose Sainik for wardrobe because it is cheap, carpenter-native, QR-verifiable, and “BWP insurance vs MR.” Those are **real procurement facts**. They are **not** enough once Club Prime, Classic Marine, Greenply 710 Marine, and HDHMR are on the table.

| Argument for Sainik | Why it fails this wardrobe |
|---|---|
| “It’s Century waterproof” | Official page: **IS 303 BWP general-purpose**, not marine IS 710. The “710” in the name is the trap retailers repeat. |
| “Carpenter always uses it / it’s on the truck” | That is **availability**, the same force that delivers fakes and unstamped “710”. Spec the SKU; then confirm stock. Do not write the default around tomorrow’s truck. |
| “Bedroom is dry, so economy BWP is enough” | Plinth mopping + monsoon still exist, but the **controlling risk is 90 in hinged leaves**: cups, sag, 0.8 mm Idria telegraph. Quora cluster: Sainik/Ecotec **alternate-core / outsourced**. MouthShut: visible **core holes** on economy MR used in wardrobes. Club Prime publishes **core composed** + screw >150 kg; Sainik publishes **neither**. |
| “HDHMR/Boilo is overkill / carpenter can’t cut it” | Then use **Club Prime or Greenply Marine ply**, not a step down to Sainik. HDHMR is the CNC alternate, not the only upgrade. |
| “Wardrobe area is large, save money” | One niche, three doors. Sheet delta vs Club Prime is real; **rehanging 90 in leaves at year 3 is larger**. Reviews: year 3–7 cost is rework (gappy core, 16 mm, two hinges, junk fittings), not skipping Architect. Club Prime is the workhorse premium, not Architect. |
| “QR + 10-year termite warranty” | QR is **authenticity hygiene** (keep it). It is not a core-gap X-ray. GLP ply is **not** a building termite treatment. |

**When Sainik is acceptable:** live quotes show Club Prime / Greenply Marine / HDHMR **cannot** arrive in 7–14 days; every sheet **QR-passes**; edge stamp **IS 303 BWP** (never a fake IS 710 story); **19 mm** (not 16); cut-face **no gaps**; **5 Sensys** per leaf; **1 mm E3 ABS** all edges including rod holes. That is a **contingency**, not the lock.

## 4. Kitchen: why Club Prime (ply) / Boilo (HDF) — not Sainik

Kitchen shutters are a **small area**. The prior rec put **Boilo first**. After reviews: Boilo has **brochure density**, not a Karnataka 5-year diary; **plywood is what on-site carpenters actually cut well**.

**Written primary = Club Prime 19 mm (IS 710 stamp)** because this job is likely a **Bhadravati carpenter**, not a Bengaluru CNC line. Composed core + calibration matches 0.8 mm Latte + 2 mm ABS + daily cups. Greenply 710 Marine is the same idea in the other major mill.

**Boilo 18 mm remains a full alternate**, not a demotion to “if desperate”: if the fabricator **shows a sample** of 0.8 mm + 2 mm ABS on Boilo without chips/bubbles, it is the better **homogeneous** shutter. Do not print IPIRTI 1-year or IS 5509 on the client PDF unless the reports are in the file.

**Sainik as kitchen default is rejected:** IS 303 BWP ? marine; alternate-core cups on daily-cycle doors; saving a few sheets here is the wrong economy.

## 6. Fabrication rules (non-negotiable)

- Photograph **ISI + IS number (303 vs 710 vs 10701 vs 12406) + CML** on every sheet edge. Century: **QR before unload**.
- Kitchen: laminate **both faces**; **2 mm E3 ABS** full perimeter; SS304.
- Wardrobe: **1 mm E3 ABS** all edges including shelf fronts and rod holes; 18–19 mm sides (no 16 mm).
- Reject chronic under-thickness, face patches, failed QR, core gaps at the cut.
- Site termite treatment of the **building**. Board GLP is not soil treatment.
- Spares: 1 laminate sheet each code, 10 m ABS each, 2 recessed pulls, 1 Sensys pair, 1 lock.
