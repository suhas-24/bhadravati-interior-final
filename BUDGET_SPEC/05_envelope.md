# 05 — Envelope Defense: Dust, Smoke, Sound, Vibration + Flooring (track R-Envelope)

**Status:** COMPLETE · strategy: seal tight, filter what enters, absorb what remains.

## 1. Window sealing ladder (cheapest → best)

| Rung | What | Cost EST | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **EPDM rubber + brush pile seals** on aluminium slider meeting faces & frame gaps | ₹30–60/ft material | kills whistling gaps, big dust reduction |
| 2 | **Neutral-cure MS-polymer sealant** on perimeter gaps (paintable, stays flexible, low VOC vs silicone which off-gasses acetic acid & never paints) | ₹350–500/tube, 2–3 tubes | permanent air-seal |
| 3 | Latch/compression upgrade + door bottom sweeps | ₹200–400/door | presses seals shut |
| 4 | **Secondary glazing: acrylic sheet insert** on clip/L-frame inside reveal (poor-man's double glazing) | ₹180–300/sqft DIY | real dB cut on traffic noise + dust barrier; validated by DIY builders (r/DIY acrylic insert projects report major street-noise drop) [38] |
| 5 | Full DGU aluminium window replacement | ₹650–1100/sqft installed | last resort — 10× rung-1 cost |

Verdict: do rungs 1–3 everywhere now (~₹4–7k whole studio), rung 4 on the bedroom + study windows first (sleep/work quality), skip rung 5 unless structural change happens anyway.

## 2. Textile defense

- **Triple-weave blackout curtains ≥280 GSM**: SS Furnishings "Deam Out" 280 GSM satin triple-weave, ₹685/panel (7 ft door size), machine-washable [37]. Warm neutral shade (taupe/greige family matches palette).
- Realistic acoustic expectation (honest): dense fabric absorbs ~30–35% of incident noise (high frequencies, echo) — takes the edge off street noise, does NOT block industrial rumble [39]. Combined with sealed windows it meaningfully improves sleep quietness.
- Wash cadence in this environment: quarterly (dust load).

## 3. Flooring decision

**Winner: SPC rigid click-floor over existing tiles** (living/bed/study/kitchen):
- Total installed **₹110–185/sqft standard / up to ₹220 premium** [34][36]; zero demolition, zero cement-dust days (critical — renovation dust is exactly what we're fighting), laid in 1 day, walkable same day [34].
- 100% waterproof core (monsoon-safe), termite-proof, quieter underfoot than tile (attached IXPE underlay), warm-neutral feel, individual plank replacement possible [34][35].
- **Spec to demand:** 0.5 mm wear layer, 5–6 mm core, attached IXPE underlay; brands: Action Tesa, Mikasa (Greenlam), Pergo, Welspun, Square Foot [35]. Mid-tone oak-grey decor hides dust between sweeps.
- Runner-up: matte vitrified 600×600 (₹100–220 installed) — cheaper material but adds demolition (₹15–25/sqft), 3–5 days of mess, grout-mold maintenance in humidity [34]. Choose only if doing full rework anyway.
- Bathrooms: leave existing floor; anti-skid matters more there.

## 4. Sound + vibration kit

| Item | Pick | Cost EST |
|---|---|---|
| Rug + pad (living) | low-pile flatweave + thick felt/rubber pad (pad does the damping) | ₹2.5–5k |
| Acoustic wall panels | PET-felt panels behind bed headboard wall (NRC ~0.4–0.75 class) | ₹250–600/panel ×4–6 |
| Appliance isolation | rubber anti-vibration pads under washing machine/fridge | ₹300–600 |
| Wardrobe fixing | chemical anchors + neoprene washers (no metal-wall buzz path) | in joinery spec |
| Door | bottom sweep + seal | ₹200–400 |

Expectation-setting: this kit + curtains + window seals ≈ noticeable high-frequency/reverb reduction and buzz elimination; low factory hum persists. Don't oversell dB numbers — comfort gain is real but partial.

## 5. Air purification (soot control)

Sizing rule: for ~378 sqft × 9.5 ft ≈ 3600 cu ft (~102 m³), 5 air changes/hr → **CADR ≥ 350–400 m³/h** target.
- **Primary pick:** Xiaomi Smart Air Purifier 4 class (CADR ~400 m³/h, ₹10–12k EST) or Philips AC1215 (CADR ~270, smaller room coverage) — verify current listing before purchase.
- **Budget backup (evidence-backed): DIY Corsi-Rosenthal box** — box fan + 4 MERV13 filters, ₹4–6k build; peer-reviewed and university studies show performance rivalling commercial purifiers at a fraction of price; the agent track retrieved dedicated cost/performance studies [40]. Great as a second unit for the bedroom at night.
- Filter budget: genuine HEPA set annually ₹1.5–3k; CR-box filters ₹800–1200/yr.
- Behavioral: run purifiers on auto; keep windows closed during visible smog/dust hours; cross-ventilate briefly during clear morning windows.
