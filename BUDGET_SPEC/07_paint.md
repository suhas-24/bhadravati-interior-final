## 1. The decision that matters most: washability class

In an industrial-belt home the wall finish is a **maintenance surface**, not just a color surface. Soot + fine dust deposit on walls; cleaning frequency will be monthly wipe-downs. That makes **scrub resistance (cycles)** and **anti-fungal additive package** the two specs to buy for — sheen stays matte/low-sheen per locked concept.

**Buy rule:** ≥5000 scrub cycles class, anti-fungal/algae additive, low-sheen. In Birla Opus ladder: **Calista Ever Wash** (premium pick) or **Style Color Smart** (economy pick). Luxury tier = One Pure Elegance Matt if budget allows.

## 3. Primer & putty

| Slot | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Putty | **Acrylic-based white cement putty** (Birla White Wallcare / Birla Opus One Pro), 2 coats | bonds to plaster, resists flaking vs pure acrylic-only putties on humid walls |
| Primer | **Interior primer tinted toward NN9074** (Calista Pro White or One Pro) | tinted primer = fewer topcoats needed = saves money |
| Sealer (smoke) | if old smoke stains bleed, one coat of **stain-blocking primer** (Asian Paints Tractor Sealer class or oil-based primer spot-primer) | soot is oily; water-based alone can let it bleed through |

## 6. Primer & putty (agent verdict, upgrades §3)

- **Putty: white-cement based, NOT acrylic** — Birla White WallCare (~₹900–1,050/40 kg) or Birla Opus Wall Care Putty (₹588–668/10 kg). Cement putty is denser/less porous → resists moisture uptake at 85% RH and survives repeated wet-wiping; acrylic putty stays soft and blisters in humid smoke-laden air (reserve for dent-filling only).
- **Primer: 1 coat water-based cement primer** (Opus Wall Primer ₹180–240/L) + **solvent-based stain-blocking primer ONLY on soot patches** — never topcoat aged soot directly, it bleeds through.
- Application protocol: putty cures 48h+; walls dry 7 days post-monsoon before painting; sugar-soap wash → dry → spot-prime → feather topcoat on soot zones.

## 7. Application notes for smoky/dusty environment

1. Cadence from the agent pass: microfiber dry-dust monthly; damp wipe quarterly (kitchen + switch zones); full mild-soap wash 2×/year — clean soot off before it ages >6 months (aged soot bonds permanently).
2. Two putty coats sanded fine → tinted primer → 2 topcoats.
3. Never: abrasive pads, bleach, solvents on emulsion (uneven sheen polishing).
4. Keep 1 L tinted leftover per shade for touch-ups.
5. Kitchen splash zone behind hob: ceramic tile dado instead of painted wall.
6. Envelope sealing (window beads + door sweep) halves the wash load more than any paint upgrade.
