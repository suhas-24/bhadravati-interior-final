## 2. Cove lighting verdict (honest)

**Skip coves.** In constant-dust air, horizontal cove shelves collect soot film within weeks and need ladder-cleaning monthly; the "premium feel" gain is small vs maintenance pain. Spend instead on CRI-90 downlights + one quality floor/table lamp for evening layering. (If insisted: sealed-profile 24V strip with end-caps, never open LED tape.)
