**Cheapest-sensible combo (spec):**
- Kitchen: carcass + shutters **Sainik 710 19mm**, loft band may be HDHMR if sealed edges.
- Wardrobe: carcass **Sainik 710 or MR ply on the dry side**, shutters **HDHMR 18mm** (flatness for Idria Oak grain) — or full 710 if quotes converge.
- Internal shelves: 12mm ply; back panel 6mm ply rabbet-fixed (not MDF).
- Chemistry notes: BWP = phenolic resin bond survives boiling water 72h (IS:710 test); termite treatment = boron-based salts in core glue-line; prefer E1/E0 emission class or Greenply Gold CARB for bedroom air quality.

## 4. Carcass construction spec (3-door wardrobe)

- Leaves: 457/457/458 × 2286 mm — vertical Idria Oak grain, 18mm HDHMR/ply core, PVC 1mm edges both faces.
- Hanging rod height: 1000–1100 mm AFF for shirts; top shelf ≥1800 mm for infrequent; drawers bottom zone ≤600 mm reach.
- Back: 6mm ply in rabbet + screwed to wall studs @ 400mm centers — **anti-vibration**: use 2-part chemical anchor or nylon sleeves so metal-to-wall contact doesn't transmit factory vibration as buzz.
- Mirror: 4mm with safety film (IS 2553 class) inside a leaf.
- LED sensor strip: optional later — wiring conduit during build even if light deferred.
