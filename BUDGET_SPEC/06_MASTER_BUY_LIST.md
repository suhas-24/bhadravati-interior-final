## Cross-checks for quotes (anti-scam)
- Carpenter quote must itemize: board brand/grade/thickness per IS mark, laminate code, edge thickness, hinge model, ₹/sqft basis (front area), GST separate [17].
- Plywood invoice must show IS:710 mark + CM/L number [15].
- Electrical: RCCB+SPD on incomer, earthing verified, licensed electrician sign-off.
- Paint: tinted-primer + 2 topcoat system written into quote; leftover 1L per shade.
