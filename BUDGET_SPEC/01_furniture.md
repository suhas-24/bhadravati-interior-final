## 1. Sofa — by construction, not looks

| Rung | Pick | Specs | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget ₹12–15k | Local carpenter: kiln-dried/teak frame + custom cushions | **spec ≥32 kg/m³ seat foam** (cheap 24–28 sinks in ~2 yrs), tight-weave performance fabric | ₹12–15k |
| **Recommended** | **Wakefit Napper Fabric 3-seater** [3] | termite-resistant **neem solid wood** frame, polyester upholstery, 194×85×85 cm, foam fill, knock-down delivery+install, 3-yr warranty (frame/mechanism 3 yr; fabric/foam/sagging 1 yr) [6] | **₹20,299** (Omega Grey Mini; velvet variants ₹32.3k MRP-discounted) |
| Premium ₹35k | IKEA MANNARP Gunnared beige [7] | washable cover (dust win), 10-yr warranty, high backrest | ₹34,990 |

**Real-user validation:**
- Flipkart buyers (490 ratings, 4.4★): "really worth the money… fabric is very good in quality… no issues after a month" [4]
- ⚠️ Counter-signal: one LinkedIn consumer post — Napper Plus fabric **faded in patches within a year indoors**, support initially dismissive [5]. Mitigation: pick mid-tone grey/beige (our palette anyway), avoid direct sun placement, keep warranty card + unboxing video.
- Dust logic applied: NO white bouclé, NO deep-tufted velvet (dust traps); tight-weave mid-tone wins.

## 2. Mattress — heat + durability first

| Rung | Pick | Why | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget <₹10k | Kurlon Dual 4–5″ flip | 60-yr brand, Sheela Foam-owned, try in-store; 4″ only if sleeper <75 kg [8] | ₹7–10k |
| Value | Wakefit DreamPod / Sleepyhead BodyIQ (Duroflex-made, best South-India service) | HR foam breathes better than memory foam in warm rooms; 10-yr warranty class [8] | ₹12–16k |
| **Winner** | **SleepyCat Latex Hybrid 8″ queen** [8][9] | latex comfort layer = coolest material class, dust-mite resistant (industrial allergy bonus), HR base support, "best buy-it-for-life under ₹20k", 10-yr warranty, 100-night trial | **₹18–20k** |

**Warranty fine print that matters (all brands):** "10-year warranty" = full replacement year 1 only, pro-rated credit after; body impression must exceed 1.5″ to qualify; one stain can void it → use a waterproof protector from day 1 [8].
"Orthopedic" is an uncertified marketing word in India — medium-firm feel (6.5–7.5/10) is the actual medical guidance [8].
Latex longevity note: ask for GOLS certificate on latex layers, else it may be blended [8].

## 3. Bed frame

**Solimo Petra solid sheesham queen (teak finish)** — ₹14.5–17.5k current, historically hits **₹11,999–14.5k** in sales (set price alert) [10]. Solid wood > engineered for creak resistance under vibration; metal frames squeak without rubber-washer bolts and rust near bathrooms [11]. **Skip box storage** — under-bed cavities collect industrial dust and are never cleaned; choose clean-leg design, vacuum underneath weekly with robot/manual.

## 4. Study setup ≤₹15k

- **Chair: Cellbell Desire C104 mesh** — ₹2.9k EST; 105 kg rating, metal base, breathable mesh (sweat-free in warm climate), tilt-lock; validated across buyer guides as best strict-budget pick [1][2][13]. Upgrade path later: Da URBAN Merlion / Green Soul Jupiter-class when budget allows.
- Desk: engineered-wood top + metal legs (1200×600 mm) or carpenter-built matte laminate top matching palette — ₹3.5–6k.
- Total ≈ ₹7–9k, leaving headroom for the task lamp in `03_electrical.md`.

## 5. Coffee table & TV unit (size/material confirmation only)

- Coffee table: round matte Ø700–800 × H400–450 mm — engineered top or solid rubberwood, matte finish per locked spec; ready-made ₹2–4k or in wardrobe-carpenter scope.
- TV unit: floating 1400–1800 mm slate-grey laminate — **carpenter-build alongside wardrobe/kitchen** (same boards, edges, labour mobilization = cheapest route); ready-made equivalents don't match the locked 80236 DW finish reliably.

## 6. Bedding

100% cotton fitted sets, 200–300 TC, warm neutrals (taupe/greige): Solimo fitted sets hit **₹626–920** on sale cycles [12]; buy 2–3 sets (weekly wash cadence in this environment). Avoid microfiber (static attracts dust).
