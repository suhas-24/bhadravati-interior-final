## 3. Ventilation strategy for dust + smoke

Honest engineering take: **exhaust-only is wrong here.** Running exhaust fans in dust/soot-laden air depressurizes the flat and pulls more dusty air IN through every gap. Strategy:
1. Bath exhaust: yes (moisture must exit) — 150 mm with **gravity shutter flap** (stops backdraft dust), Havells/Crompton class ₹800–1400 EST.
2. Kitchen: rely on chimney/recirculating mode or window-positioned exhaust used *only while cooking*, flap closed otherwise.
3. Fresh air: windows sealed + filtered (see `05_envelope.md`), not unfiltered infiltration.
4. Chimney: budget auto-clean 60cm (Faber/Elica/Glen entry models ₹8–12k EST) if cooking is oily — it protects Latte shutters and NN9074 walls from soot film; highest-leverage kitchen appliance after the hob itself.
